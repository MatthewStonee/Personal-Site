How the plans differ

  • Dining. A has an enclosed dining room with wide openings to the kitchen and living room. B has an open dining area beside the kitchen.
  • Kitchen. A has a 7-ft island beside a column. B has an 11-ft island and a small pantry.
  • Primary bath. A has a longer double vanity and a second door from the entry hall. B has a wider glass shower and is reached only from the bedroom.
  • Bedroom 2. A opens to the living room through double doors and has its own dressing nook, closet and Bath 2. B opens off a short hall shared with Bath 2 and a linen closet.
  • Plan C is a different, smaller unit (≈1,140 ft²). Its two bedrooms sit at opposite ends, each with a walk-in closet and its own bath. The open living and dining room has corner windows, the kitchen island seats three, and the balcony opens off the living room.

About these models

Plans A and B were traced from the listing's 3D marketing renderings. Each image was perspective-corrected onto a 1-ft grid and scaled to the listed 1,604 ft², so room sizes are estimates, roughly ±1 ft. Their exterior windows and patio doors aren't visible in the renderings, so those positions are inferred. Plan C was built from your dimensioned floor plan, so its walls, doors and windows follow the drawing and its rooms show the labelled sizes.
